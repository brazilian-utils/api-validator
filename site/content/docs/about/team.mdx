Many more people have contributed over the years. Each library lists them on GitHub, under its contributors. How the project started is in the FAQ, under [How did it start](/about/faq/#how-did-it-start).

## Join the team

Maintainers come from contributors. Someone who keeps sending good pull requests and reviews is invited by the current maintainers of that library. The [community guide](/about/community-guide/) shows where to start.
