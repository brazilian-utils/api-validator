Brazilian Utils is made by volunteers, in the time they have. [Hyan Mandian](https://github.com/hyanmandian) created it in 2018. Since 2025 it grows together with [Cumbuca Dev](https://github.com/cumbucadev), a community that teaches programming through open source: Camila Maia, Maria Antônia Maia, Nilton Pimentel and Leonel Sanches da Silva come from it.

<Team />

Many more people have contributed over the years. Each library lists them on GitHub, under its contributors.

## Join the team

Maintainers come from contributors. Someone who keeps sending good pull requests and reviews is invited by the current maintainers of that library. The [community guide](/about/community-guide/) shows where to start.
